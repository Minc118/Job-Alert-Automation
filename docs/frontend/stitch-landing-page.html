Privacy and control

Login vs. Data Access

Logging in only creates your account. Gmail connection is a separate, optional step requiring specific read-only permissions.

Backend-only Analysis

Email parsing and AI analysis happens securely on our servers, not in your browser, ensuring data integrity.

No Direct DB Access

User data is siloed. Support staff cannot access your raw emails, resume data, or parsed jobs directly.

Resume Handling

Your uploaded resume is used strictly as context for the AI matching algorithm and is never shared.
